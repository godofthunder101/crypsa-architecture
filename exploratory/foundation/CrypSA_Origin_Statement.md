## CrypSA Origin Statement

CrypSA, the Cryptid Server Architecture, was created by Beau Wells as a conceptual architecture for persistent digital universes.

The model emerged from an attempt to rethink the foundations of multiplayer world design. Instead of treating online worlds as continuously simulated server state, CrypSA treats them as canonical structures that evolve through **validated canonical events** and are reconstructed locally by observers.

Its core ideas include:

* minted canonical identities
* deterministic structural genomes
* invariant-protected canonical event history
* validated event-driven evolution
* observer-relative simulation
* lens-based interpretation layers

CrypSA was developed as an open architectural model intended to explore a different future for online worlds: one in which universes can be persistent, replayable, reconstructable, and preservable beyond the lifespan of any individual server implementation.

The architecture has since evolved into a structured model separating:

* truth (validation and canonical events)
* translation (adapters)
* interpretation (lenses)
* experience (observer simulation and UI)

This repository serves as the canonical public record of the architecture’s origin, authorship, and ongoing development.
